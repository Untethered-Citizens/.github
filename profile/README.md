# Untethered Citizens

**Tools for living on your own terms. Made by enthusiasts, for each other.**

## Who we are

Untethered Citizens is a home for hobbyists and enthusiasts who build software for fun, and to make everyday life a little easier.

We make small apps and tools for the things real people deal with every day: keeping track of your health, getting organized, being more productive, or just having a good time. Each one starts the same way: someone had an everyday problem, built something to solve it, and shared it.

We're not a startup, and we don't make professional or enterprise software. There's no sales team, no pricing page and no "book a demo". Just everyday people building useful things, and sharing them.

## Free and open source

Everything we make is **free and open source**. That means two things:

- **Free to use.** No price, no trial, no subscription, no "premium" tier.
- **Open source.** The code that makes the app work is public. Anyone can read it, learn from it, fix it, improve it, or build their own version. Our projects use the [Apache 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) licence, which allows all of that.

Why it matters to you:

- **You don't have to take our word for it.** You don't have to trust a privacy policy; anyone can check exactly what an app does with your data.
- **It can't be taken away.** If we ever stop working on a project, it doesn't vanish. Anyone can pick it up and keep it going.
- **Anyone can make it better.** Every user is a possible contributor, so good ideas don't have to wait on a company's roadmap.

(One exception: our names and logos aren't part of the licence. If you publish your own version of an app, give it its own name.)

## Bring your own AI

Some of our apps use AI. Most AI apps send your requests through the company's own servers and charge you a monthly subscription for it. We do it differently:

- **You bring your own AI key.** You sign up with an AI provider such as Anthropic, add your key to the app, and pay the provider directly for what you use, usually pennies.
- **The app talks straight to the AI.** There's no server of ours in the middle, so nobody else sees your requests.
- **The AI only does what AI is good at:** understanding what you mean, estimating, and conversation. Everything else (the maths, the totals, your history) is plain, predictable code on your device.

## What you can count on

Every project here follows the same promises:

- **No accounts.** Your data stays on your device.
- **No ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **No subscriptions.** Where AI helps, you bring your own key.
- **Honest AI.** When a number is an AI estimate, the app says so.

## Get involved

- **Try it.** Download an app and use it in your everyday life. That's what it's for.
- **Say hello and share ideas.** Our [community discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Untethered-Citizens/discussions) are open to everyone: ask a question, suggest an idea, or show how you use an app. A free GitHub account is all you need.
- **Tell us what's broken.** Bugs and "this confused me" are welcome too. Open an issue on the project's GitHub page, or use the project's support page.
- **Help build it.** You don't need to be a professional developer. Testing, writing, design, and ideas count as much as code. Each project's page explains how to contribute.
- **Become an Untethered Citizen.** Help shape where this goes. People who contribute regularly are invited to join the organization as members.
