# Untethered Citizens

**Tools for living on your own terms.**

We make small, honest software that you own and control:

- **No accounts.** Your data stays on your device.
- **No ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **No subscriptions.** Where AI helps, you bring your own key and pay the AI provider directly, usually pennies.
